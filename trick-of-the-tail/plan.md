# Empirical study protocol: real-data adaptation of *Trick of the tail*

Status: frozen implementation protocol derived from [sources/trick-of-the-tail-proper-plan-v2.md](sources/trick-of-the-tail-proper-plan-v2.md) (7 November 2026) under the explicit user instruction: **"skip the simulation part, focus on the real data only,"** incorporating all accepted pre-freeze refinements from [plan-critique.md](plan-critique.md). The earlier exploratory synthetic + EUR package is archived in [archive/v1-time-varying-tails/](archive/v1-time-varying-tails/README.md).

## 1. Scope adaptation and preserved claim boundaries

This study implements the complete **real-data empirical inference and chronological extreme-quantile forecasting** design of [sources/trick-of-the-tail-proper-plan-v2.md](sources/trick-of-the-tail-proper-plan-v2.md) for Shuo Li and Liuhua Peng's *Time-Varying Tails: Estimation, Inference, and Risk Measurement* ([sources/2611.06005v1.pdf](sources/2611.06005v1.pdf), Sections 4–9 and Remarks 5.3, 6.1 and 7.2):

- **Omitted by user scope instruction**: Section 6's synthetic Monte Carlo validation and power experiments (500-replicate null-size runs across synthetic IID Pareto, scale-drift Pareto, IID Student-t, AR-Pareto, stationary stochastic volatility, stationary GARCH-t, and prefix-fitted GARCH/GJR synthetic histories, plus the three synthetic power curves and the two corresponding simulation graphs).
- **Retained pivotal calibration for real-data inference**: Remarks 5.3 and 6.1 and Section 5 compute critical values and empirical $p$-values via `B = 5,000` IID unit-scale Pareto paths $(1 - U_t)^{-\gamma_0}$ ($\gamma_0 = 0.3$, $U_t \in [0, 1)$) per distinct `(n, m, h, J, kernel, support, grid, quadrature, floor)` key, plus `1,000` bootstrap resamples of the stored calibration draws to report critical-value Monte Carlo uncertainty.
- **Explicit claim-boundary consequence**: Because the local synthetic null-size gate in Section 6 was intentionally skipped, baseline stability outcomes are reported strictly as **nominal paper-calibrated pivotal inferences** (with raw and 9-slot Holm-adjusted $p$-values at nominal $\alpha = 0.05$), never as locally certified 5% familywise error guarantees. Narrative Section 3 (*Can the test control false alarms?*) explicitly highlights that in Li & Peng's own simulations (Table 3), raw GARCH processes can over-reject at ~13–14% at nominal 5% prior to volatility filtering.

## 3. Numerical, filter, multiplicity and failure specifications

1. **Data and exposure orientation**:
   - Fixed window `1999-01-04` through `2025-12-31`, hash-pinned in [data/source-manifest.json](data/source-manifest.json).
   - EUR (`DEXUSEU`, copied unchanged from `archive/v1-time-varying-tails`) and GBP (`DEXUSUK`) are USD per foreign unit; JPY (`DEXJPUS`) is yen per USD and inverted to `1.0 / quote` before differencing so `loss_t = -100 * diff(log(usd_per_foreign_unit))` represents USD holding losses across all three currencies (`6,769` losses each: `2,516` in `<= 2008-12-31` prefix, `4,253` in `2009-01-02` to `2025-12-31` scored window, with elementwise-identical `4,253` scored dates verified before joint resampling).
2. **Exact IEEE-754 support and quantile rules**:
   - Standardized kernel distance uses `dist = (n * u - t) / (n * h)` with strict open support `abs(dist) < 1.0` so rational boundaries (`n = 2500, u = 1.0, h = 0.10`) include exactly `250` observations rather than `251`.
   - Weighted empirical CDF left-inverse targets use `((n - m) * total_weight) / n` when integer `(m, n)` defines $q = m/n$, eliminating +1 ULP threshold shifts at `n = 3000, m = 200`.
3. **`arch==8.0.0` filter specification (retrospective interior inference only)**:
   - Input: signed percentage returns `r_t = -loss_t` (`rescale=False`); negative returns correspond to positive USD losses and activate GJR asymmetry `1{eps_{t-1} < 0}`.
   - Both SLSQP attempts (`tol=None, options={'ftol': 1e-9, 'maxiter': 1000}`) are always executed and retained: Attempt 1 uses `arch`'s deterministic grid starting values; Attempt 2 uses `[mean(r), 0.03*var(r), 0.07, 0.89, 7.0]` (GARCH) or `[mean(r), 0.03*var(r), 0.05, 0.04, 0.89, 7.0]` (GJR). Among valid attempts, Attempt 2 is selected iff `ll_2 > ll_1 + 1e-10`; otherwise Attempt 1 is selected.
   - Validity requires `convergence_flag == 0`, `omega > 0`, `alpha >= 0`, `beta >= 0`, `alpha + gamma_lev >= -1e-9` (allowing negative `gamma_lev` on JPY while preserving variance positivity), `persistence = alpha + 0.5*gamma_lev + beta < 1 - 1e-6`, `nu > 2.05 + 1e-5`, finite positive volatilities, and reconciliation against `numpy_garch_recursion` (`ewma_backcast(r - mean(r))` and `eps = r - mu_hat`) within `rtol = 1e-10, atol = 1e-10`.
4. **Multiplicity, forecasting, uncertainty and failure accounting**:
   - **Holm family**: Preserves all 9 baseline slots (`{EUR, GBP, JPY} x {Raw, GARCH |z|, GJR |z|}`); any failed slot receives internal `p = 1.0` and adjusted decision `N/A`, with step-down $\tilde{p}_{(k)} = \min(1, \max_{j \le k} (9 - j + 1) p_{(j)})$ at nominal $\alpha = 0.05$. Six EUR one-factor deviations (`m = 150, 250`, `h = 0.075, 0.125`, `J = 10, 30`) are recalibrated at `B = 5,000` each and reported descriptively across all three EUR targets.
   - **Past-only forecasts**: Arms A, B, C and D on raw signed losses (`u = 1, h = 0.10, m = 200, q_t = m/n_t, p in {0.01, 0.025}`). Both expanding and rolling-2,500 anchor Arm A and Arm B's fixed shape to the frozen `<= 2008-12-31` prefix (`n_0 = 2,516`). Pinball loss is $\rho_\tau(y - Q) = (\tau - \mathbf{1}\{y < Q\})(y - Q)$ ($\tau = 1 - p$, strict violation $y > Q$). All `4,253` origins remain in denominators; any missing origin/arm marks the contrast incomplete without subsetting or carry-forward.
   - **Forecast uncertainty & diagnostics**: Paired circular moving-block bootstrap (`5,000` resamples, primary `L = 20`, sensitivity `L = 10, 60`, `.025/.975` `method='linear'`), primary contrast `mean(loss_C - loss_B)` on EUR `p = 0.01` expanding. Kupiec UC and Christoffersen IND likelihood ratios treat `0 * log(0) = 0` and return `N/A` for Christoffersen IND unless all four marginal transition totals (`n_0., n_1., n_.0, n_.1`) are positive.
5. **Seeds, pre-freeze pilot isolation and 13-asset sequence**:
   - Master seed `20261107` via `PCG64(SeedSequence([20261107, phase, group, sub, rep]))`: phase `10` disjoint pilot (`<= 2008-12-31` prefix and phase-10 Pareto draws only; zero `2009–2025` outcomes touched), phase `20` pivotal calibration (`group = uint32(spec_sha256[:8])`), phase `50` cutoff (`sub = 1`) and moving-block (`group = 100..310`) bootstraps.
   - Numerical budget: `1,800`s and `4 GiB` RSS ceiling (3x pilot projection + 60s reserve).
   - Shared 9 headings and 13 single-panel assets: `01` scope table, `02` filter/HAC diagnostics table, `03` EUR raw path/band, `04` EUR GARCH path/band, `05` EUR GJR path/band, `06` 9-test Holm table, `07` EUR rolling-250 violations graph, `08` EUR cumulative `C - B` loss graph, `09` EUR A/B/C/D score table, `10` multi-series/window `C - B` interval graph, `11` supporting FX/window/block table, `12` EUR robustness table, and `13` calibration cutoff & workload table.
